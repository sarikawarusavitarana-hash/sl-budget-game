Frequently Asked Questions

Sri Lanka Budget Game (Educational Simulation)

What is this game for?

This game helps players understand public-budget trade-offs in Sri Lanka. It is an educational simulation, not an official budget forecast.

Are the outputs official government estimates?

No. Outputs are simulation results built from simplified assumptions and published context data.

Where do policy and model assumptions live?

  • Policy narrative content: content/policy-content.json
  • Model parameters and assumptions: content/model-config.json

How can contributors validate changes?

Run node scripts/validate-configs.js and complete at least one full browser playthrough.

Where can I find contribution and deployment guidance?

  • docs/contribution-guide.md
  • docs/beta-readiness-checklist.md
  • docs/github-pages-deployment.md
